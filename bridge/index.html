Blue is being pulled (tension) Red is being squashed (compression) Brighter means closer to snapping
How it works

Every joint is a small mass and every member is a stiff spring. A member snaps when the force in it passes its strength. Members are weaker when squashed than when pulled, because long thin struts buckle.

Triangles are strong because a triangle cannot change shape unless one of its sides stretches. A plain road sags like a rope; add triangles and it becomes a truss. That is why real bridges, cranes and roofs are full of them.
